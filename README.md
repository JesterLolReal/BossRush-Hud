# BossRush HUD

A premium **client‑side Fabric mod** for **Minecraft 1.21.11** that provides a real‑time, fully‑customizable HUD for hardcore boss‑challenge runs and livestreams.

---

## Features
- **Boss tracking** – Warden, Elder Guardian, Wither, Ender Dragon (configurable kill requirements).
- **Death counter** – counts Hardcore deaths and resets boss progress.
- **3‑D player‑face badge** – draggable badge showing your skin and username.
- **Drag‑and‑drop HUD editor** – move the boss HUD and player badge independently; preset anchors, scale sliders, and resolution‑independent positioning.
- **Animated defeat overlay** – red “X” animation and desaturated icons when a boss is defeated.
- **Stream mode** – contrast‑boosted layout, compact view, auto‑hide outside Hardcore.
- **Full Mod Menu integration** – configure all options without leaving the game.

---

## Installation
1. Download `bossrush-hud-1.0.0.jar` from the **Releases** page.
2. Place the JAR in your Minecraft `mods` folder (`.minecraft/mods`).
3. Launch Minecraft with the **Fabric Loader** (1.21.11).
4. Open **Mod Menu → BossRush HUD** to adjust settings or press **Right Shift** (configurable) to open the HUD position editor.

---

## Usage
- Press **H** to toggle HUD visibility (re‑bindable).
- Right‑click the HUD while the editor is open to drag it.
- Use the Mod Menu sliders to change scale, opacity, and animation speed.
- In **Stream Mode**, the HUD adopts a high‑contrast palette suitable for OBS captures.
